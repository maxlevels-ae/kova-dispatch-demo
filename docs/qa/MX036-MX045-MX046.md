## Limits and production safeguards

Browser playback evidence does not establish human-perceived device audibility, mobile silent-mode behavior or closed-browser delivery. Responsive Chromium tests are not physical iOS/Android device tests. SOS uses truthful device calling/manual sharing; no unimplemented emergency dispatch is claimed. Markets without published content retain neutral empty content until an administrator publishes it.

Production verification is read-only apart from authentication sessions. No production seed, fabricated GPS, wallet adjustment, content publication or operating-country activation is performed. The pre-deployment live country was JO and must remain unchanged. Exact deployed commit, health and live browser verification are reported after deployment.
