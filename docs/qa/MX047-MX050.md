# MX-047 through MX-050

## Behavior

- Send Package defaults pickup to reported GPS. Bring me a package defaults the recipient to the authenticated account and dropoff to reported GPS or a saved place. Manual endpoints survive mode changes and navigation. Permission denial never labels the country map center as an exact user location.
- The server's operating country supplies currency and local telephone parsing. Jordanian `0793088001` normalizes to `+962793088001` on the order without overwriting the account. A nonlocal/incomplete account contact requires an order-specific local handover contact. International account identity remains intact.
- Independent Taxi/Delivery `enforceZones` settings default on for legacy records. Off bypasses custom polygons only; sourced country containment, pricing readiness, hours and matching remain enforced. Simple zone switches preserve geometry/history and enable the zone's city. Drawing/editing lives under Advanced Zone Management.
- Country-specific Delivery Payments settings default to wallet only. Cash records delivery-fee collection independently from merchandise COD. Server-computed amounts, currency precision, explicit confirmation, immutable references and MongoDB transactions prevent double posting. Cash orders never debit customer wallets or credit wallet earnings for money the courier physically holds.
- Admin records actual courier remittance, merchandise payout and separate fee/merchandise refunds with receipts. Courier retained earnings and platform commission remain separately identifiable. Cash cancellations have no automatic penalty; collected money requires an auditable refund. Wallet cancellation behavior is preserved.
- Card checkout remains unavailable without a verified operational gateway. COD is optional, limited, and available for Send Package only; the booking account holder is its payee. These controls do not initiate bank transfers or external payouts.
- Delivery booking/tracking reuse original rider vehicle, pin and navigation assets, the full map, rounded bottom sheets and existing typography. Customer details/chat/proof are expandable. Tracking uses authorized recent server GPS, original vehicle markers, pickup/dropoff routing, stale timestamps and reconnect/poll recovery. Route-service fallbacks are labelled approximate.
- Shared MapView now initializes supplied markers after asynchronous map configuration loads. Tracking camera padding follows the sheet height. A hidden duplicate customer map was removed; Admin/courier investigation maps remain.

## Production safeguards

No production seed, destructive migration, wallet conversion, GPS injection, payment-provider activation, country activation or content publication. The current production country is JO. Pre-deployment fingerprints cover country configuration and rider wallet accounts; post-deployment browser/health verification checks the exact commit and unchanged fingerprints.

Cash and COD remain disabled in existing production configuration until an administrator deliberately enables them. Human physical handover, cash collection, real-device tracking and external card processing are not verified by local browser automation.
